MULTI-AGENT SYSTEMS2026.07.15–08.15

Persona-to-Scene: Personalized Interior Generation

Built collaborating AI agents that design personalized rooms from descriptions of a person’s identity, personality, and daily habits. The system turns these descriptions into room layouts, furnishings, and interior images.

Shown: an art student’s painting space and an astronomy-loving physics teacher’s living room.
